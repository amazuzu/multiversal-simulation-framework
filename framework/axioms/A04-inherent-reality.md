# A04 — Pain still counts

**Question:** If this life is a simulation, is my joy or pain fake?

**Answer:** No. From the inside, experience is **full**. “Simulation” says where the world runs. It is not a discount coupon on feeling.

**Next door:** [R08](../rules/R08-equivalence-of-realities.md) says a whole sim-world isn’t worth less than a “base” world. [M01](../measures/M01-ontological-parity.md) is the checklist so you don’t sneak a ranking back in.

## Advanced

$$\text{Reality}_{\text{local}}(n) = \frac{\text{what } n \text{ experiences}}{\text{what this world's physics allows}} = 1$$

**Read it as:** measure a life in the units of the world it happens in. The score is always **full**—never “half real because it’s a sim.”

| Symbol | Means |
|--------|--------|
| $n$ | One person (or subject) inside a world. |
| $= 1$ | Experience counts at full strength. |
