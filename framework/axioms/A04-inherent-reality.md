# A04 — Inherent reality

## Q&A

**Question:** If my life is a simulation, is my experience “less real”?

**Answer:** No. From the inside, your life is fully real for you. “Simulation” is a label about where the world runs, not a discount on feeling.

**Equations:** [FORMULAS.md § A04](../FORMULAS.md#a04--local-reality-normalized-to-unity)

## Implications

- “Simulation” does not downgrade phenomenal reality for the experiencer inside that world.
- External labels (base vs sim) are metadata; local phenomenology follows local physics.
