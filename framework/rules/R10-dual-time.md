# R10 — The disc and the movie

**Question:** Is time a frozen whole, or a road we walk?

**Answer:** Both can be true in one story. **Outside**, the cosmos can be a finished object—past and future already in the shape, like a film on a disc. **Inside**, you still live forward, minute by minute, and those minutes can hurt. A frozen map does not make your afternoon fake ([A04](../axioms/A04-inherent-reality.md)).

## Advanced

$$\Delta T = \int_{\text{path}} \sqrt{-g_{\mu\nu}\, dx^{\mu} dx^{\nu}}$$

**Read it as:** the “length” of a life is the length of a path through spacetime, like distance along a road. $g$ is the geometry (how distances work there). The whole history can sit still as a shape, while $\Delta T$ is still the time a person *lives* along their path.
