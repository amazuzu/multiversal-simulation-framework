# R12 — A cosmos with a serial number

**Question:** Could a whole universe be one giant number?

**Answer:** As a **story**, yes. Write the laws as a finite recipe; old logic tricks turn recipes into integers. Some tales then say: that integer is a **prime**, and the entire history is just the shape of that prime—still, not a machine in a basement. Existence still means “the rules don’t contradict” ([A01](../axioms/A01-non-contradiction.md)), not “we found Earth’s prime.”

Fits the “endless but carved” tier in [R11](R11-constrained-infinity.md).

## Advanced

$$G = 2^{s_1} \cdot 3^{s_2} \cdot 5^{s_3} \cdots$$

**Read it as:** turn a finite recipe of symbols $s_1, s_2, s_3, \ldots$ into one integer by using primes (2, 3, 5, …) as shelves and the symbols as how many times each shelf is used. Different recipes get different numbers. A *story* then says some universe is “the prime $P$ that encodes that recipe.” That is a label, not a proof that Earth has a prime ID.
