# R11 — Infinite, but not “anything goes”

**Question:** Can a universe be endless and still have rules?

**Answer:** Yes. There are infinitely many primes, and still no even prime bigger than 2. Big does not mean every pattern shows up.

| Tier | In plain words |
|------|----------------|
| 1 | A box: finite, or hard caps (a lifespan, a budget). |
| 2 | Endless, but the rules cut most patterns out. Optional “one prime names the world”: [R12](R12-universe-prime.md). |
| 3 | Every consistent structure exists (the widest dream). |

Worlds of different tiers can still touch ([R06](R06-overlap-blending.md)).

## Advanced

$$\text{allowed patterns} \subset \text{all imaginable patterns}$$

**Read it as:** $\subset$ means “a smaller set inside a bigger one.” A world can be infinite (tier 2) and still leave most patterns out, the way primes sit inside the integers and skip every even number above 2. Tier 1 is finite. Tier 3 is “every consistent world.”
