# Q02 — Are you actually choosing?

We **don’t know**.

**Question:** If the world is math—or a sim—were your choices already written?

**Answer:** Maybe the path is fixed and freedom is a feeling. Maybe branches exist, and a mind that models itself has to **walk** them; nobody outside can skip ahead without living the steps (that’s the “you can’t fast-forward the movie” idea). A third story: the whole self leans on one branch. We don’t crown a winner.

Time as a finished disc vs time you live: [R10](../rules/R10-dual-time.md).

## Advanced

$$|\Psi\rangle = c_1 |\text{branch}_1\rangle + c_2 |\text{branch}_2\rangle + \cdots$$

**Read it as:** a choice can be written as several open branches at once, each with a weight $c_i$. One story says you cannot skip ahead: any shortcut that predicts the choice without running the inner loop is longer than just living it. Another story says the weights were fixed all along. The formula only draws the branches. It does not decide which story is true.
