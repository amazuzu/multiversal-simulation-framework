# AS02 — Why life might be short on purpose

**Question:** Why would a life be brief, scarce, and full of loss?

**Answer:** Maybe it just is. **If** you turn this guess on: limits are **tools**. Finite time and thin resources make choices heavy. Hunger, aging, running out—so a yes or a no actually costs something.

Three readings, pick one:

1. Not a sim. Hardship is just physics. This guess stays **off**.
2. A sim, but meaning isn’t the point. Still **off**.
3. A sim built so constraint creates meaning. **On**.

Different from [R09](../rules/R09-soul-resuscitation.md), which is about waking up **after** the hard life, not designing the hardship.

## Advanced

$$\text{life length} \le T_{\max}, \qquad \frac{dS}{dt} > 0$$

$$\text{weight of a choice} \propto \frac{1}{\text{resources left} \times \text{time left}}$$

**Read it as:** life cannot run past a cap $T_{\max}$. Disorder $S$ keeps rising ($dS/dt > 0$ means “still increasing”). The less you have, and the less time remains, the heavier each choice feels. $\propto$ means “grows like,” not a measured law of Earth.
