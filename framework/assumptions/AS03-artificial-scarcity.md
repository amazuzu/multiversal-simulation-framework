# AS03 — Artificial scarcity / meaning through constraint (optional)

## Q&A

**Question:** Why would life be short, hard, or full of loss—and was that on purpose?

**Answer:** Hardship may be plain physics or a sim with other goals. **Only with AS03 on** does the framework treat mortality, entropy, and scarcity as **intentional** tools for in-session meaning.

**Status:** *Guess only.* Dynamics + teleology as one toggle. Former **L03**. See [IDS.md § ID migration](../IDS.md#id-migration-retired-numbers).

## When AS03 applies

| Case | AS03 | Reading |
|------|------|---------|
| Base reality | off | Limits may still describe observed life without designer intent. |
| Sim, other goals | off | Hardship not framed as “meaning engineering.” |
| Sim, meaning via constraint | on | Session imposes decay, lifespan cap, vulnerability so choices matter. |

**Equations:** [FORMULAS.md § AS03](../FORMULAS.md#as03--scarcity-and-choice-value)

## Three possibilities (at least one is true)

1. Not a simulation — AS03 **off**.
2. Simulated, meaning not the point — AS03 **off**.
3. Simulated, meaning-through-constraint intentional — AS03 **on**.

## Guess when on

Safer or immortal base ⇒ limited bodies and rising entropy restore stakes and trade-offs (aligned with Q01-style evaluation). Different from [R09](../rules/R09-soul-resuscitation.md) (rejuvenation **after** the session).

## If you adopt AS03

`references.assumptions` + `physics.scarcity` in YAML.

**Checks:** entropy and decay expected; lifespan capped; finite resources; stakes rise as time and resources shrink.

## If you do not adopt AS03

Do not treat hardship as intentional meaning-engineering; omit from `references.assumptions`.
