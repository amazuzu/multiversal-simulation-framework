# AS01 — The memories you don’t get

**Question:** Why don’t we remember past lives, or a world “above” this one?

**Answer:** We don’t know. **If** you turn this guess on: anything that would spoil a fair test—who you were, that you’re being watched, that you can’t really die—stays locked until the life ends. Not deleted. Just not in the room.

A story that uses the lock: life as a blind audition ([Q01](../questions/Q01-existential-purpose.md)). If the person upstairs knows they’re immortal and graded, every choice becomes acting. Forgetting makes the scene honest. Still a guess.

## Advanced

$$\text{memory you can use} = \text{all memory} \setminus \text{the locked part}$$

**Read it as:** $\setminus$ means “take away.” During the life, origin, past lives, and “you are in a test” stay in the locked part. They still exist. They just don’t steer today’s choices. In symbols: the chance of an action, given only active memory, does not depend on the locked set.
