# Axioms (simple)

**Axioms** are the starting points: if you use this framework, you agree these ideas fit together. They are not optional toggles in YAML—you adopt the set A01–A04 when you use the framework at all.

| ID | In plain English | More |
|----|------------------|------|
| **A01** | A universe only “exists” in this model if its rules don’t contradict themselves—consistent physics means consistent logic, not “must look like Earth.” | [A01](../axioms/A01-non-contradiction.md) |
| **A02** | There is one kind of underlying stuff: information/math patterns. Matter and mind are not two separate substances. | [A02](../axioms/A02-mathematical-monism.md) |
| **A03** | Consciousness is a system that models and updates itself—“I” in a feedback loop, not a magic soul particle. | [A03](../axioms/A03-self-reference-consciousness.md) |
| **A04** | Your lived experience is fully real **to you**, even if someone outside calls your world a simulation. | [A04](../axioms/A04-inherent-reality.md) |
