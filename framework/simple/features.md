# Features (simple)

**Features** are optional mechanics for “this universe runs inside another.” Turn them on in YAML only when your model needs them (`references.features.simulation`).

| ID | In plain English | More |
|----|------------------|------|
| **FS01** | Inner “you” and outer “you” can stay linked by entanglement—not a brain upload copy. | [FS01](../features/simulation/FS01-session-entanglement.md) |
| **FS02** | Our world might be hosted on a parent-world quantum computer (story only, not proven). | [FS02](../features/simulation/FS02-quantum-computer-host.md) |
| **FS03** | Unobserved places stay vague until someone looks—like a game that only renders what you see. | [FS03](../features/simulation/FS03-deferred-rendering.md) |
