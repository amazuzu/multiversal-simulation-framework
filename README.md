# multiversal-simulation-framework

## Preface

**This is not a source of truth.** Nothing here is presented as established science, scripture, or final philosophy. It is a personal working noteset: one person’s attempt to think through **universe and life** in conversation with **Google Gemini**, organized so ideas that clash are flagged instead of smuggled in as fact.

The author (**Taras Biletskyi**) is a **software developer**, not a professional in the universe, life, physics, or mathematics—just someone **curious about life** trying to reason in the open.

The **mission** is modest: **understand the universe and life a bit better**—nothing more. We already have **enough tools** for that (logic, evidence, math, existing science and philosophy, and honest conversation); this repo is one way to organize the attempt, not a claim that something essential was missing.

Treat the framework as a **speculative model** and a consistency exercise. Adopt, adapt, or discard pieces freely; verify anything important against primary sources and your own judgment.

**Comments, change requests, and criticism are very welcome**—issues, pull requests, or plain conversation. Point out errors, gaps, or contradictions; that is how this noteset improves.

---

An attempt to describe **life**, **consciousness**, and the **universe** as a single story built only from ideas that **do not contradict each other**—existence as consistency ([A01](framework/axioms/A01-non-contradiction.md)), local reality, optional simulation narratives, and open questions where we genuinely do not know.

Themes overlap with well-known work (e.g. mathematical universe, simulation hypothesis, IIT); see **[framework/INFLUENCES.md](framework/INFLUENCES.md)** for author tags and provenance.
